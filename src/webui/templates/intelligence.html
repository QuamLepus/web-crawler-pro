Plan → discover → crawl → extract → verify → report

  1. Plan. The question becomes sub-questions and a page budget.
  2. Discover. Candidates come from your own index first, then the topic-discovery providers — never from a third-party search API.
  3. Crawl. Robots, politeness and per-host rate limits apply exactly as they do for a normal job.
  4. Extract. A recipe extracts the claim; if none matches, the page's structured data is used, and the field is marked healed.
  5. Verify. A claim needs one source, a strong claim needs two. Unverified claims are separated from the answer.
  6. Report. Markdown with inline citations and a signed manifest.

What this cannot do

  • It does not answer questions about the open web in general — only about pages it can reach and that are in scope.
  • It does not invent a number when no citation exists; you get the plan and a refusal instead.
  • The confidence score is a heuristic over source agreement, not a statistical guarantee.
