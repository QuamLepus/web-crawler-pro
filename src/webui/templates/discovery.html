How ranking works

  1. The topic is expanded into several search queries.
  2. Results are fetched from the configured provider — or from your own index, which costs nothing.
  3. Each candidate domain is scored on relevance, authority, diversity and whether it looks like a directory or a shop.
  4. Near-duplicate sites are collapsed so the shortlist is distinct.
  5. You approve. Only then does anything get crawled.

Cost control

Preview ranks without crawling. Approval is per-job, and you can reject individual candidates before anything is fetched.

Pages are only consumed once you approve — that is the step that touches the quota.
