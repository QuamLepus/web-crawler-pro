How to read this

  • authority — best in-index signal we hold for the host, or an index-depth proxy, and we say which.
  • coverage — how much of the host we actually hold (log-ish: 100+ documents saturates).
  • freshness — decay over 180 days since the newest fetch.
  • licence — from each page's own metadata. Unknown scores 0.4, never “free”.
  • robots — share of recorded fetches that robots.txt allowed.
