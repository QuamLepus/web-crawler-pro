Questions people actually ask

Is the free tier real?

A thousand pages a month. Enough to build a level-0 index and keep it fresh — which is the part that replaces paid search.

What does a search cost?

Nothing per query once the index is built. There is no metered search behind this product.

Do I need a credit card?

No. Overage only appears if you cross the plan ceiling, and you can cap it.

Can I run it myself?

Yes — one FastAPI process plus Redis, and the index builds from datasets that are free to redistribute.
