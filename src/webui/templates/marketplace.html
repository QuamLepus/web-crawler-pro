{# ── how it works ── #}

Selling

  1. Run a crawl to completion. The job record is stamped with a data_sha256.
  2. Create a listing pointing at that job ID.
  3. Buyers browse the catalogue title, description, record count and licence.
  4. Free acquisitions create a signed manifest; paid purchases are unavailable until payment settlement is integrated. No dataset bytes are transferred by this console.

  5. Leave a rating so the next buyer knows.
